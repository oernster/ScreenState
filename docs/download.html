Setup program (.exe)

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning when you run it: here is why.

Run it and choose Install. It installs for your account only, so Windows never asks for administrator rights. The same program updates, repairs and uninstalls.

  1. Capture your desktop from the manager, save the profile and sign out and in again to watch it come back.

To remove it, uninstall from Settings > Apps. Your profiles live apart from the program, so they stay unless you tick the box that removes them too.
